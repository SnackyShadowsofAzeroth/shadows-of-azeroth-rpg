# M&M Race Lore and Class Eligibility

Primary reference: [Monsters & Memories races](https://monstersandmemories.com/races). Use the source's names and lore as the canon anchor for future character, setting, and story work. The summaries below are paraphrases, not replacements for the linked page.

## Race descriptions

- **Human** — The continent's most numerous and varied people. Four cultures shape common human backgrounds: seafaring Ba’Haara, noble Tarellians, tradition-bound and corruption-prone Easthymn, and nomadic Sojourners. Individual humans differ widely.
- **Gnome** — Brilliant, inventive truth-seekers with distracted or curt habits; often gentle and kind, though some turn their talents toward dark gods.
- **Deep Gnome** — Cataclysm-changed descendants who became fearless meta-scientists and explorers of the multiverse, dreamscapes, astral planes, and Spirals. Their curiosity drives contact with the surface.
- **Dwarf** — A people with origin myths tied to titan blood and the Galdri Range. Craft, drink, astronomy, and exploration matter to them; they are tough, quick-tempered, loyal fighters and makers.
- **Deep Dwarf** — Dark kin shaped in the Deep among corrupted stone. Their spirit-binding creations, cold precision, and desire to exploit the surface and its Fonts of Life make them calculating conquerors.
- **High Elf** — The Cilindrine, remnants of the Calafrey Conglomerate. Long-lived, magically and militarily accomplished, proud, doctrinal, and insular; diplomacy is growing amid internal factional conflict.
- **Wood Elf** — The Faelewyn, changed after the Cilindrine denied them shelter and the Fae of the Branch intervened. Reclusive but hospitable, deeply tied to forest life, and divided over whether to return to the Fae Realm.
- **Deep Elf** — The Ail’trist, once hunted and driven underground, where the Shadow warned them of catastrophe. They adapted to the Deep, mastered or coexisted with demonic forces, reclaimed an ancient city, and remain formidable and secretive.
- **Ogre** — Created by Storm Coast giants as warriors, then left homeless after catastrophe. Their northern society is martial and stratified; they are fierce, disciplined, fearless, and known as soldiers, mercenaries, and craftsmen.
- **Halfling** — Eth-ur's oldest people, born of the Fonts of Life and attuned to the living world. Their ancient lore-keepers and storytellers are now mostly peaceful farmers and settlers, with adventurous wanderers still among them.
- **Troll** — Survivors who freed themselves from the Spiral Lords and withdrew to difficult hunting grounds. Resilient, adaptive, cunning, distrustful, and predatory; skilled hunters, trappers, and survivalists who are often watched with suspicion.
- **Goblin** — Feared for greed and deception, but valued for sensing secrets, hidden entrances, and lies. Perceptive opportunists from the labyrinthine Heap, with a sharp and often sick humor.
- **Var’nai** — Lizardfolk abducted and altered into living weapons by necromantic captors, who escaped to Eth-ur and became attached to its waters. Their harsh origins coexist with a disciplined culture and enhanced intellect.

### Current patch exception

The race page's Deep Gnome list omits Bard, but the later official 0.30.6.0 patch notes say Deep Gnome Bard is now in. Keep Bard available for Deep Gnome unless a newer official source changes this. The page's Var’nai class list is recorded for lore/reference; new creation remains disabled until the user supplies an in-game stat line.

## Writing guidance

- Preserve the culture-specific details and tensions above; do not reduce a race to one personality or treat group traits as universal.
- Distinguish source canon from SoA inventions. Add new local detail only when it fits the source themes, and do not rewrite named canon, history, or affiliations.
- Do not assign a race a class outside its listed access. Human class access may depend on origin/start location.
